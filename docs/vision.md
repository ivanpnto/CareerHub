# Vision
---

## Product Vision

CareerHub aims to become a centralized platform where students and early-career software developers can manage every stage of their professional journey from a single place.

Instead of relying on scattered spreadsheets, notes, emails, bookmarks, and job boards, users can organize companies, job opportunities, applications, interviews, tasks, and career-related information in a structured and efficient way.

CareerHub is designed with long-term growth in mind. The initial version focuses on providing a reliable and intuitive foundation for job search management, while future versions will progressively incorporate advanced productivity features, analytics, and AI-powered assistance that deliver real value without replacing user decision-making.
---

## Purpose

Searching for a job involves much more than submitting applications.

Students and early-career professionals often need to keep track of companies, job opportunities, application statuses, interviews, deadlines, notes, and personal tasks. This information is frequently scattered across spreadsheets, calendars, emails, notes, and multiple job platforms, making the process difficult to organize and maintain.

CareerHub exists to centralize the entire job search process into a single platform, providing users with a structured, organized, and reliable workspace where they can manage every step of their professional journey with confidence.
---

## Target Users

### Primary Users

CareerHub is primarily designed for:

- University students looking for internships or their first full-time position.
- Recent graduates starting their professional careers.
- Early-career software developers actively searching for new opportunities.

These users often manage multiple applications simultaneously and need a centralized workspace to organize companies, job offers, interviews, tasks, and personal notes.

### Secondary Users

Although not the primary focus of the MVP, CareerHub can also be useful for:

- Career changers entering the software industry.
- Self-taught developers preparing for their first professional role.
- Professionals who prefer a structured approach to managing their job search.
---

## Goals

The primary goals of CareerHub are:

- Provide a centralized workspace to manage the entire job search process.
- Help users organize companies, job offers, applications, interviews, and tasks in a clear and structured way.
- Reduce the risk of losing important information, deadlines, or interview schedules.
- Offer an intuitive and consistent user experience that requires minimal learning.
- Establish a solid and extensible foundation for future features without compromising simplicity in the MVP.
---

## Non-Goals

The first version of CareerHub is intentionally focused on delivering a solid and reliable foundation. Therefore, the MVP will not include:

- AI-powered features or automated recommendations.
- Integration with external job platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, Indeed, InfoJobs).
- Resume or cover letter generation.
- Calendar synchronization with third-party services.
- Email notifications or push notifications.
- Real-time collaboration or shared workspaces.
- Social networking or recruiter-oriented features.
- Advanced analytics and reporting.
- Mobile applications.
- Multi-user organizations or team management.
---

## Success Metrics

The MVP of CareerHub will be considered successful if users can:

- Manage their complete job search process from a single platform.
- Easily track the status of every application.
- Organize companies, interviews, and tasks without relying on external tools.
- Quickly identify upcoming interviews and pending tasks.
- Navigate the application with a simple and intuitive user experience.
---

## Guiding Principles

CareerHub is developed following a set of principles that guide every product and engineering decision.

### User Value First

Every feature should solve a real user problem before adding complexity.

### Simplicity Over Complexity

The simplest solution that satisfies the requirements is preferred over unnecessary complexity.

### Build for Evolution

The architecture should support future growth without introducing premature optimization or overengineering.

### Consistency Matters

The application should provide a predictable and consistent user experience across all features.

### Quality Over Quantity

A small number of well-designed features is more valuable than many incomplete or poorly integrated ones.

### Maintainability First

The codebase should remain clean, modular, and easy to understand so that the project can evolve over time.

### Learn Through Good Engineering

CareerHub is built by applying professional software engineering practices, emphasizing thoughtful design, documentation, testing, and continuous improvement.
